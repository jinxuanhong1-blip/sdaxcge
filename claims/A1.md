# A1 — TACSTD2 vs immune remains negative after purity adjustment (TCGA + OncoSG)

- **User number:** A1
- **Source:** User PPT 2026-08-17 (金炫宏)

## Claim (as stated by user)
In TCGA-LUAD and OncoSG lung adenocarcinoma cohorts, tumor `TACSTD2` (TROP2) expression is
**negatively** correlated with immune infiltration / immune signatures, and this negative
association **persists after adjusting for tumor purity**. In other words, the anti-correlation
is not merely a dilution artifact driven by non-tumor cell content.

## Public test plan
1. Pull LUAD bulk RNA-seq expression + clinical + purity estimates for both cohorts.
2. Define immune readouts: CIBERSORTx/xCell immune fractions, ESTIMATE ImmuneScore, GEP/T-cell-inflamed
   signature, CD8A/CD8B, GZMB, PRF1, IFNG.
3. Compute Spearman ρ between `TACSTD2` and each immune readout.
4. Repeat with **partial Spearman correlation controlling for tumor purity** (ESTIMATE purity,
   ABSOLUTE, or TCGAbiolinks purity). Also stratify into purity tertiles and check the sign is
   preserved within strata.
5. Report ρ and p before vs after purity adjustment; the claim holds if ρ stays negative and
   significant after adjustment.
