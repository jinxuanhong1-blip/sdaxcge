**Fig. 2 | KL cell lines express more Tacstd2, Cldn4 and an epithelial tight-junction program than KP cell lines.**

Bulk RNA-seq of mouse lung-cancer cell lines (GEO GSE137244; Deng et al., PMID 34142094). Expression is log2(FPKM + 1). Five KrasG12D;Lkb1 (KL) libraries are compared with five KrasG12D;Trp53 (KP) libraries. The normal-lung library (GSM4073826) is held out. These are bulk cell-line measurements, not single-cell data.

**a,** *Tacstd2*. **b,** *Cldn4*. Each point is one library (n = 5 per genotype). The horizontal line is the mean of those five libraries. Δ is mean(KL) − mean(KP): *Tacstd2* +3.24, *Cldn4* +5.57. Every KL library lies above every KP library. The *Tacstd2* point on the baseline is B6AL10-3 (log2(FPKM + 1) = 0).

**c,** Epithelial tight-junction core: the published per-library mean of log2(FPKM + 1) over 18 structural genes (*Cldn1*, *Cldn3*, *Cldn4*, *Cldn7*, *Ocln*, *Marveld2*, *Marveld3*, *Tjp1*, *Tjp2*, *Tjp3*, *F11r*, *Jam2*, *Jam3*, *Cgn*, *Cgnl1*, *Crb3*, *Ildr1*, *Lsr*). Δ = +1.63. Every KL library lies above every KP library. The score is taken from the published library means and is not recomputed here.

**d,** Library-level values for genes that have a per-library table. The upper block is higher in every KL library than in every KP library: *Cldn6* (not part of the 18-gene core), *Cldn4*, *Cldn7*, *Cldn3*, *Cldn1*, *Tacstd2*, *Ocln* and *Tjp1*. The lower block is higher in KP: *Cldn5*, *Cldn18* and *Cldn2*. Δ at the right is mean(KL) − mean(KP). *Marveld2*, *Marveld3*, *Tjp2*, *Tjp3*, *F11r*, *Jam2*, *Jam3*, *Cgn*, *Cgnl1*, *Crb3*, *Ildr1* and *Lsr* contribute to the score in **c** but have no per-library values in the source table, so they are not drawn and no points were imputed.

P values are two-sided exact Mann–Whitney tests on the ten libraries. When the groups do not overlap, U = 25 if KL is higher and U = 0 if KP is higher, and P = 0.00794 (2/252). That is the smallest two-sided P available at n = 5 versus 5. The five KP columns are libraries B6AL10-1 through B6AL10-5, not five independent KP lines.
