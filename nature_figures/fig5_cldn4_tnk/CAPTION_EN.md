**Fig. 5 | Malignant CLDN4 percent-positive is inversely associated with the T/NK fraction across four concordant cohorts.**

**a**, Malignant CLDN4⁺ cell percentage versus T/NK fraction in 65 tumour units. GSE123902: donors, marker-defined malignant cells, n = 13 (Laughney 2020; normals excluded). GSE131907: samples, author-defined malignant cells from tumour-bearing sites, n = 21 (Kim 2020). GSE205335: patients, author-defined malignant cells, advanced ICI, all subtypes, n = 22. GSE189357: patients, marker-defined malignant cells, n = 9 (Zhu/Wang, AIS–IAC). The grey line is an ordinary least-squares guide through the 65 plotted units. Annotated statistics are the four-cohort DerSimonian–Laird pool of Fisher *z*-transformed Spearman coefficients: ρ = −0.53, P = 1.6 × 10⁻⁵, n = 65 units, I² = 0%. A Spearman correlation of the 65 superimposed units is −0.55. The annotated ρ is the cohort-level pool.

**b**, T/NK fraction for the within-cohort CLDN4 Q1 (low; n = 19) versus Q4 (high; n = 16). Quartiles were assigned by within-cohort rank and then combined. Boxes show the median and interquartile range; whiskers extend to 1.5 × the IQR; every unit is drawn. Medians are 0.43 and 0.12 (Δ median = −0.31). Rank-biserial r = −0.72. Two-sided P = 2.9 × 10⁻⁴. Colours match **a**. GSE189357 contributes 3 Q1 and 2 Q4 units; a within-cohort test was not reported for that cohort and is not shown.

**c**, Cohort Spearman ρ (CLDN4 percent-positive versus T/NK fraction) and the pooled estimate. Point area scales with the number of units. `tnk_singles.tsv` does not contain single-cohort confidence intervals. The diamond is the pooled ρ (−0.53) with its reported 95% CI (−0.70 to −0.31). I² = 0%. Cohort rows: GSE123902, ρ = −0.66, P = 0.014, n = 13; GSE131907, ρ = −0.52, P = 0.015, n = 21; GSE205335, ρ = −0.44, P = 0.043, n = 22; GSE189357, ρ = −0.60, P = 0.088, n = 9.

**d**, Mean log fold-change (Q4 versus Q1) for malignant gene-set families in the concordant-4 count matrix, with the reported standard error. n = 34 units (18 Q1, 16 Q4); one Q1 unit had no count matrix and is omitted here but kept in **a** and **b**. Negative values are lower in the CLDN4-high quartile. IFN, logFC = −0.58, P = 0.002 (FDR = 0.0049, 221 genes); chemokine, logFC = −0.96, P = 7.7 × 10⁻⁵ (FDR = 3.1 × 10⁻⁴, 25 genes); MHC-I/APM, logFC = −0.78, P = 0.010 (FDR = 0.013, 21 genes); tight junction, logFC = 0.04, P = 0.54 (FDR = 0.54, 194 genes). P values are the reported family tests. Gene counts are the sizes of the gene sets. The sample size is the 34 units.
