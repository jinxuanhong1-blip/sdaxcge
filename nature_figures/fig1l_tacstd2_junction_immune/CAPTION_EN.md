**Fig. 1l | Tumour TACSTD2 tracks a Hallmark apical-junction score; neither TACSTD2 nor that score tracks the T/NK fraction.**

Malignant pseudobulk expression and the T/NK fraction were taken from the locked concordant-4 human NSCLC cohorts. Each point in the forests is a cohort Spearman coefficient. Horizontal lines are Fisher *z* 95% intervals. The diamond is a DerSimonian–Laird random-effects pool of those coefficients, back-transformed to ρ. Labels on the figure are ρ rounded to two decimals. These three relations were estimated separately: a TACSTD2–junction association and a TACSTD2–T/NK association do not imply a junction–T/NK association.

**a**, Tumour TACSTD2 versus the Hallmark apical-junction score (mean log2(TMM-CPM+1), 194 genes). GSE123902, 13 donors, ρ = 0.48 (95% CI −0.09 to 0.82), P = 0.094. GSE131907, 21 samples, ρ = 0.27 (−0.19 to 0.63), P = 0.24. GSE205335, 21 patients, ρ = 0.61 (0.25 to 0.83), P = 0.003. GSE189357, 9 patients, ρ = 0.12 (−0.59 to 0.72), P = 0.77. Pooled ρ = 0.43 (0.18 to 0.62), P = 9.8 × 10⁻⁴, n = 64 units, I² = 0%. Unrounded pooled ρ = 0.428.

**b**, Tumour TACSTD2 versus the T/NK fraction in the same units. GSE123902, ρ = 0.03 (−0.53 to 0.57), P = 0.91. GSE131907, ρ = 0.26 (−0.20 to 0.62), P = 0.26. GSE205335, ρ = 0.21 (−0.25 to 0.59), P = 0.37. GSE189357, ρ = −0.12 (−0.72 to 0.59), P = 0.77. Pooled ρ = 0.15 (−0.12 to 0.40), P = 0.26, n = 64, I² = 0%. Unrounded pooled ρ = 0.155. The estimate is weak and positive. It is not evidence that TACSTD2-high tumours are lymphocyte-poor.

**c**, The same junction score versus the T/NK fraction. GSE123902, ρ = −0.07 (−0.60 to 0.50), P = 0.83. GSE131907, ρ = −0.17 (−0.56 to 0.29), P = 0.47. GSE205335, ρ = 0.44 (0.01 to 0.73), P = 0.047. GSE189357, ρ = 0.42 (−0.34 to 0.85), P = 0.26. Pooled ρ = 0.15 (−0.20 to 0.46), P = 0.41, n = 64, I² = 35%. Unrounded pooled ρ = 0.146. Cohort signs are not the same, and the pool is compatible with no association. Panel **a** does not establish panel **c**.

The unit is the donor (GSE123902; Laughney 2020; marker-defined malignant cells; normal lung excluded), the sample (GSE131907; Kim 2020; author-defined malignant cells; tumour-origin samples with at least 20 malignant cells), or the patient (GSE205335, author-defined malignant cells; GSE189357, Zhu/Wang, marker-defined malignant cells). n is units, not cells. The locked concordant-4 table has 65 units. P4001 (GSE205335) has no malignant count column and is omitted, so every panel uses the same 64 units. Direction and P value were not used as inclusion criteria.

The junction score is MSigDB Hallmark apical junction (HALLMARK_APICAL_JUNCTION, 200 genes in the frozen set). TACSTD2 is not a member. Six set members were absent after the expression filter (count ≥ 10 in ≥ 3 units): ACTC1, ACTN3, CD209, DMP1, DSC1, NRAP. The score is the unweighted mean of the remaining 194 genes, on log2(TMM-CPM+1), with the same gene list in every cohort. CLDN4 is one of those 194 genes.

Prior malignant CLDN4 percent-positive versus T/NK (Fig. 5 / PR #780; n = 65; pooled ρ = −0.53) is Part 2. It is not redrawn here and it is not TROP2 evidence.

Extended Data, scatters: each included unit, with the cohort Spearman ρ, P and n printed above the panel. Axis limits are shared within a row. Extended Data, sensitivity: dropping CLDN4 (193 genes) leaves the TACSTD2–junction pool at ρ = 0.42 (0.17 to 0.61), P = 0.001, I² = 0%, and the junction–T/NK pool at ρ = 0.18 (−0.20 to 0.50), P = 0.36, I² = 45%. A KEGG tight-junction mean (154 genes after the same filter; TACSTD2 removed, though it was not a member) gives TACSTD2–junction ρ = 0.42 (0.16 to 0.63), P = 0.003, I² = 13%, and junction–T/NK ρ = 0.11 (−0.38 to 0.56), P = 0.66, I² = 70%. The KEGG–T/NK cohorts disagree in sign. TACSTD2 versus T/NK does not use the junction score, so that panel is unchanged by these sensitivities.

P values are two-sided and are not adjusted across the three pre-specified relations. The pooled P is normal on the Fisher-*z* scale. Hartung–Knapp *t* P values (3 degrees of freedom) are 0.038, 0.13 and 0.45 for panels **a**, **b** and **c**; they are stored in `KEY_STATS.json` and are not drawn. Full-precision coefficients are in `source_data/effects.tsv`.
