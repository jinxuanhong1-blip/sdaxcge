**Fig. 1 | TROP2-high and CLDN4-high tumor cells have fewer nearby CD8 and NK cells at short range.**

CosMx SMI non-small-cell lung carcinoma sections from He et al. (2022). Each point is one section. Colors are patients (Lung5, Lung6, Lung9, Lung12, Lung13). Squares and diamonds are replicate sections. Black bars in **a** and **b** are equal-weight means of the eight section means. The printed fold is the ratio of those means (high / low). The patient is the inferential unit: a one-sided exact sign test on five patients is P = 0.031 when every patient mean is lower in the high arm. This is an observation about neighborhood composition. It is not a causal claim and it is not a claim about immunotherapy resistance.

**a**, TACSTD2 (TROP2), section-median split among author-matched tumor cells. Mean CD8+NK neighbor counts. Equal-weight ratios 0.455 at 10 µm and 0.670 at 20 µm (shown as 0.46× and 0.67×). High < low in 8/8 sections and 5/5 patients.

**b**, CLDN4 top versus bottom quartile among marker-defined tumor cells, from a separate neighbor-count table. Mean CD8+NK counts inside the field of view. Equal-weight ratios 0.460 at 20 µm and 0.574 at 40 µm. The same direction holds at 60 µm (0.649) and 80 µm (0.703); those radii are not drawn. High < low in 8/8 sections and 5/5 patients at each of these four radii.

**c**, Section high/low ratios for the median split used in **a**, extended to 50 and 100 µm, and the same median split for CLDN4. Fractions above each column are sections with ratio < 1. Diamonds are ratios of means. TACSTD2 remains lower in 8/8 sections at 10 and 20 µm and is not concordant at 100 µm (4/8). CLDN4 median split is concordant at 10 µm (ratio of means 0.494; 8/8 and 5/5) and is not concordant at 50 µm (0.810; 4/8) or 100 µm (0.912; 4/8).

**d**, Counts per million of GZMB, PRF1, NKG7, and IFNG in CD8 or NK cells that sit near CLDN4-high versus CLDN4-low tumor (section-median split). Open symbols, 50 µm; filled symbols, 100 µm; bars are medians. GZMB CPM is higher near CLDN4-high tumor in 8/8 sections at 100 µm (median ratio 1.18). PRF1, NKG7, and IFNG are not uniformly higher or lower. Fewer cytotoxic neighbors is not the same pattern as lower effector transcripts in the cytotoxic cells that are present.

A cited summary ratio of 0.36 at 50 µm and 0.52 at 100 µm is not plotted. The repository tables do not contain the section-level values for that summary. The section-level median-split ratios at those radii are the points in **c**.

No spatial coordinate table is in the repository, so this figure has no tissue map. Neighborhood counts were not recomputed.
