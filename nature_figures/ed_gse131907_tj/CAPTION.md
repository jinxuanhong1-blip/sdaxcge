# Extended Data figure caption

**Tight-junction scores increase between TACSTD2 Q1 and Q4 in GSE131907, and TACSTD2 abundance does not track T-cell fraction.**

**a**, Within-patient change in the claudin score (mean of *CLDN1*, *CLDN3*, *CLDN4* and *CLDN7*; log1p of counts per 10,000 UMIs) for epithelial cells in metastatic lung (tL/B), lymph node (mLN) and pleural effusion. The contrast is TACSTD2 quartile 4 minus quartile 1 inside each patient. Filled circles are that paired difference. Open circles are the same score after a cell-level linear residual on *EPCAM*, *KRT8*, *KRT18* and *KRT19*. Dashed lines mark the unweighted means, 0.542 for the claudin score and 0.398 for the residual. All 10 of 10 patients are positive on both scores (exact two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank *P* = 0.00195). The median claudin difference is 0.556. Grey numbers are epithelial cells in each arm; quartile arms are the same size, and the mean is not weighted by cell count. *n* = 10 patients.

**b**, Mean paired difference. The *CLDN4*-only row is a different contrast: epithelial cells with brain metastasis excluded, TACSTD2 Q4 versus Q1, *n* = 21, mean 0.851, median 0.835, 21/21 positive, exact *P* = 9.5 × 10⁻⁷. That row is one gene, not a module, and the released table does not contain the 21 patient-level differences, so no interval is drawn. The middle block is one shared contrast (*n* = 10): *CLDN1/3/4/7* mean 0.542; epithelial control *EPCAM/KRT8/18/19* mean 0.514; *KRT8/18/19* alone mean 0.489; *CLDN1/3/7* mean 0.417; claudin score residualized on the epithelial control, mean 0.398; pre-specified 12-gene core mean 0.292. The 12-gene core is *CLDN1*, *CLDN3*, *CLDN4*, *CLDN7*, *OCLN*, *TJP1*, *TJP2*, *TJP3*, *F11R*, *CGN*, *MARVELD2* and *CRB3*. The bottom row is the pre-specified core on author-malignant cells with a median split (*n* = 20, mean 0.131, 18/20 positive, exact *P* = 9.5 × 10⁻⁶). Bars are 95% *t* intervals of the patient-level mean and are drawn only for rows whose patient-level differences are in the source table. Every *n* = 10 contrast in which all 10 differences are positive has the same exact signed-rank *P* = 0.00195; the panel compares effect size. *TACSTD2* is not a member of any score. Epithelial controls are open squares.

**c**, Pre-specified test on author-malignant cells: percentage of cells with *TACSTD2* UMI > 0 versus the T-cell fraction of the sample. One point is one patient (*n* = 21). Colour is the malignant-cell fraction. Spearman ρ = −0.157, *P* = 0.50. The grey line is ordinary least squares and is not the test. Partial Spearman ρ given the malignant-cell fraction is −0.013 (*P* = 0.96). Author-annotated malignant cells in this matrix are metastatic (mBrain, tL/B, mLN), not primary lung.

**d**, Spearman ρ for pre-specified patient-level tests, with 95% percentile bootstrap intervals (5,000 resamples of patients). The highlighted row is panel **c**. Primary tumour epithelium (tS) versus T-cell fraction is ρ = −0.188, *P* = 0.60, *n* = 10. The same predictor versus CD8 fraction is ρ = 0.588, *P* = 0.074 (the point estimate is positive). The orange row is the smallest nominal depletion *P* in the search: author-malignant brain metastases, *TACSTD2* fraction with UMI ≥ 2, versus the cytotoxic CD8 fraction (ρ = −0.663, nominal *P* = 0.037, *n* = 10). It is not a confirmatory result. Partial ρ on the malignant-cell fraction is −0.537 (*P* = 0.11). Across these 10 patients the cytotoxic outcome sums to 29 cells, and 2 of 10 patients are zero. Leave-one-out Spearman *P* ranges from 0.009 to 0.14. A permutation *P* for this single panel is 0.041 (4,999 shuffles). The Bonferroni 0.05 threshold for the Spearman family (1,932 tests) is 2.6 × 10⁻⁵. Open diamonds are partial ρ given the malignant-cell fraction.

Inferential *n* is the patient. The matrix contains 208,506 cells; that count is not a sample size. Dissociated counts are not a spatial exclusion test.
