## ccr-23-2566_supplementary_figure_s2_suppfs2.docx (778056 bytes)

Supplementary Figure 2. Plasma level of TROP2 predicts response to ICI. NSCLC patients treated with immunotherapy were classified as High or Low based on their baseline plasma level of TROP2 dosed using the OLINK technology. (A) Spearman correlation of the level of soluble TROP2 (sTROP2) protein with TACSTD2 tumor gene expression. (B) Proportion of patients with High and Low levels of soluble TROP2 who experienced durable clinical benefit (DCB) or non-durable clinical benefit (NDB). P value was calculated using a Chi² test.(C-D) Kaplan meier curves of the progression-free survival (C) and overall-survival (D) of NSCLC patients according to TROP2 plasma level. (E) Volcano plot representation of the gene differentially expressed between sTROP2 High and Low patients. (E) Gene Set Enrichment Analysis of the MEK activation pathway (MEK_UP.V1_UP signature) in the tumor of patients with High or Low levels of plasma TROP2. (F) Histograms of the estimated score of T gamma delta, Th1 cells and macrophages in the tumor of sTROP2 High and Low patients. P value were calculated using Wilcoxon test.
