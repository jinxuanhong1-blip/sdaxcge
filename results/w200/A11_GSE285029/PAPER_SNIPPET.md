## Methods (supplement)

Public bulk RNA from the Koh et al. ICI–RNA-seq cohort (GEO GSE285029;
PMID 40050048) was scored for TACSTD2 and CLDN4 against CD274, a compact
IFN-γ set (IFNG, STAT1, IRF1, CXCL9, CXCL10, CXCL11, IDO1, GBP1), a
classical MHC-I cassette, IL6, a compact IL-6/STAT3 set, CD8A, and the
Ayers 2017 18-gene T-cell-inflamed GEP (unweighted z-mean). The GEO series
releases an author-processed WTS matrix for n = 234 pre-ICI NSCLC tumors
and does not include RECIST, histology, PD-L1 IHC, or purity. Expression
was analyzed as log2(pmax(x,0)+1). Associations are Spearman ρ with
Fisher-z 95% CIs. TROP2-high / CLDN4-high was the top quartile versus the
bottom quartile (Mann–Whitney U). Sitting on a high PD-L1 or IFN program
was Q4×Q4 overlap versus the 25% independence expectation (Fisher exact).
An epithelial z-mean (EPCAM, KRT8, KRT18, KRT19) was used only as a
purity-like sensitivity residual.

## Results (main or supplement)

In GSE285029 (n = 234), TACSTD2 and CLDN4 co-expressed (ρ = 0.53,
p = 5.0×10⁻¹⁸) and then split on immune programs. TACSTD2 was only weakly
related to CD274 (ρ = 0.16, p = 0.016, BH q = 0.11) and was null for the
IFN-compact score (ρ = 0.10, p = 0.14), MHC-I (ρ = 0.07, p = 0.26), IL6
(ρ = −0.05, p = 0.48), CD8A (ρ = −0.08, p = 0.20), and GEP18 (ρ = 0.01,
p = 0.91). TROP2-high tumors were not enriched for CD274-high or
IFN-high quartiles (25% and 19% overlap; expected 25%) and were depleted
for GEP18-high (14%, OR = 0.38, p = 0.023) and CD8A-high (12%, OR = 0.32,
p = 0.006) tumors. CLDN4 tracked IFN-compact (ρ = 0.20, p = 0.0018,
q = 0.006), MHC-I (ρ = 0.26, p = 4.6×10⁻⁵), and Hallmark IFN-γ (ρ = 0.30,
p = 2.6×10⁻⁶). CLDN4-high tumors were enriched for the IFN-high quartile
(42%, OR = 3.05, p = 8.5×10⁻⁴) but not for the CD274-high quartile (32%,
OR = 1.60, p = 0.17); CD8A and IL6 remained null. Epithelial residualizing
removed the TACSTD2–CD274 and CLDN4–IFN-compact primary associations
(both partial p ≈ 0.12). TROP2-high tumors in this public ICI-era matrix
therefore do not sit on a high PD-L1 or high IFN program. CLDN4-high
tumors sit on an IFN / MHC-I program without being PD-L1-high or
CD8-high. This is baseline co-expression only; it does not measure
on-treatment PD-L1 induction.
