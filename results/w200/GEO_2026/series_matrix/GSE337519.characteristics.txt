!Series_title	"Single-cell transcriptomic landscape of neoadjuvant chemo-immunotherapy response in lung cancer"
!Series_summary	"This study provides a high-resolution single-cell transcriptional profile of lung cancer in the context of neoadjuvant treatment. Utilizing the 10x Genomics platform, we performed single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) on paired patient samples to investigate the cellular composition, phenotypic states, and transcriptional dynamics of the tumor microenvironment in response to the therapeutic intervention."
!Series_overall_design	"Single-cell RNA sequencing was performed on paired tumor samples obtained from a single lung cancer patient, with no biological replicates. The patient was treated with a neoadjuvant regimen consisting of three cycles of combined chemotherapy and immunotherapy. Samples were collected at two distinct time points for comparative analysis: pre-treatment (baseline) and post-treatment (following the completion of the three-cycle regimen)."
