!Series_title	"Spatial Transcriptomics Unveils Immune Microenvironment Heterogeneity and Response-related Subtypes in NSCLC Treated with Neoadjuvant Chemoimmunotherapy"
!Series_summary	"We conduct a single-arm phase 2 trial using pembrolizumab plus platinum-doublet chemotherapy to evaluate the efficacy and safety of perioperative immune-chemotherapy in patients with driver mutation-negative potentially resectable NSCLC. Further, we perform an exploratory analysis of spatial transcriptomic profiles to characterize the immune microenvironment landscape in primary tumor lesions and paired draining lymph nodes after chemoimmunotherapy, aiming to explore distinct pathological response features and potential mechanism."
!Series_overall_design	"In this single-arm phase II trial (NCT05383716), patients with resectable NSCLC received neoadjuvant pembrolizumab plus platinum-based chemotherapy, followed by surgery and adjuvant immunotherapy. The primary endpoint was major pathological response (MPR). Digital spatial profiling was performed on resected tumors and paired draining lymph nodes from 22 patients to to characterize the immune landscape of response."
