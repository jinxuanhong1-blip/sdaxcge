B7 TEER / barrier literature — CLDN4 in lung epithelium and cancer
==================================================================
Date: 2026-08-16
Scope: public papers only. Numbers copied from paper text. No invented p-values.

VERDICT
-------
1. Lung epithelium: several public primary papers measure TEER/TER or
   related barrier endpoints after CLDN4 gain/loss.
   Core set: Wray 2009, Mitchell 2011, Kage 2014, Rokkam 2011, Lee 2018.

2. Lung cancer cell lines: no public paper was found that reports TEER/TER
   after CLDN4 knockdown, knockout, or overexpression in A549, H441,
   Calu-3, H1975, or other NSCLC lines. Arabi 2024 (Front Oncol review)
   states there have been no studies examining the mechanistic role of
   claudin-4 in LUAD development.

3. Other epithelial cancers: public TER/Rb numbers exist for ovarian
   (Litkouhi 2007), gastric (Nishiguchi 2019; 4D3 antibody, not KD),
   and pancreatic (Kuwada 2019; 4D3 antibody). These are catalogued
   separately from lung.

4. Foundational non-lung TEER papers (MDCK/LLC-PK1) are included as
   context only: Van Itallie 2001, Van Itallie/Colegio 2003, Hou 2006.
