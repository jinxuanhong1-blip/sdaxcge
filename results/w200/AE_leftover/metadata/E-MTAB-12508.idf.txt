Investigation Title	scRNA-seq of CD45+ cells isolated from lungs of mice carrying KPneo tumors that received FLT3L and αCD40 (DC-therapy) or rat IgG2a isotype control
Experiment Description	Dendritic cells are key player to initiate anti-tumoral response. In this study we aimed to characterize the function of type 1 DCs during priming of CD8 T cell responses against endogenous neoantigens encoded by a KrasG12D/WT; Tp53 (KP) lung cancer model. To enhance the immunostimulatory properties of cDC1, we delivered a combination of Flt3L and aCD40 antibodies. We found that this therapy is sufficient to induce proliferation of neoantigen specific CD8 T cells and restrain growth of lung tumor nodules. scRNA-seq transcriptional profiling of the lung immune infiltrate of tumor-challenged animals with and without DC-therapy was performed to understand the molecular changes induced in DCs subsets and CD8 T cell subsets.

Protocol Description	Lungs were harvested at day 9 and digested to obtain single cell suspensions to be processed for scRNA analysis. CD45+ cells were isolated using ARIA Cell Sorter (BD). Single cell suspensions were processed for single cell analysis using Next GEM Single Cell 3ʹ Reagent Kits v3.1 (Dual Index) (10X Genomics) following manufacturer’s instructions.	Nucleic acids (polyA RNA) were extracted during single cell encapsulation and immediately barcoded, according to Next GEM Single Cell 3ʹ Reagent Kits v3.1 (Dual Index) (10X Genomics) protocol.	Libraries were constructed using Next GEM Single Cell 3ʹ Reagent Kits v3.1 (Dual Index) (10X Genomics) protocol and following manufacturer’s instructions.	Following quality controls, dual index libraries were sequenced according to manufacturer’s instruction on Novaseq6000 platform (Illumina). Sequencing protocol was: Read1=28 bp, Read2= 90bp, Index1=10 bp, Index2=10 bp.	8-weeks females C57/BL6 mice (4 mice per group) were injected with 5x10^4 KPneo cells intravenously. Lungs were harvested at day 9 post-inoculation.	Mice were administered 30 ug of recombinant Flt3L on day -2, 2, 4 , 6. On day 5, mice received a single dose of aCD40 (200ug intraperitoneally). Control mice received intraperitoneal injections of PBS on day -2,2,4,6 and isotype rat aIgG on day 6.
