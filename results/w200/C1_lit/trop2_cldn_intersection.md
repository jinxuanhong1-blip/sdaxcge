# TROP2–claudin intersection — catalog notes

This file is **not** ADC internalization kinetics. It records public evidence that TROP2/TACSTD2 sits near claudins, including CLDN4.

## Direct binding / localization

### Nakatsukasa et al., 2010 — TACSTD2, CLDN1/7 binding, CLDN4 localization

- **PMID** 20651236 | **DOI** 10.2353/ajpath.2010.100149 | *Am J Pathol* 177:1344–1355
- **Access:** `full_text` (publisher HTML)
- GDLD prevalence in Japan quoted as 1 in 31,546 (from parental-consanguinity estimate).
- EpCAM is the closest homolog: “48% identity over a range of 1500 bp.”
- Immunoprecipitation in HCE-T-15: TACSTD2 binds CLDN1 and CLDN7. Binding described as relatively higher for TACSTD2/CLDN7 than TACSTD2/CLDN1.
- Quoted: “other TJ-related proteins such as the CLDN4, TJP1, and OCLN proteins were not coimmunoprecipitated with the TACSTD2 proteins” at any detergent concentration tested.
- In situ PLA on normal cornea: positive signals between TACSTD2 and CLDN1, CLDN4, CLDN7, and TJP1; no significant signal with OCLN. Most PLA signal on plasma membrane, trace in cytoplasm, virtually none on Golgi.
- TACSTD2 shRNA lowered TER over a 1-week period vs controls. Knockdown of CLDN1, CLDN4, or CLDN7 each lowered TER.
- In HeLa, co-expression of TACSTD2 recovered CLDN1 and CLDN7 level and membrane distribution; CLDN4 level “almost unchanged.”
- MG-132 for 24 h increased CLDN1 and CLDN7, “virtually no effect” on CLDN4. Authors conclude TACSTD2 protects CLDN1/7 from ubiquitin–proteasome degradation.
- GDLD corneas: decreased expression and altered localization of CLDN1, 4, 7, ZO-1, and occludin (qualitative in retrieved text).
- **C1 reading:** TACSTD2 is required for normal CLDN4 *localization* in cornea/HCE-T, but the IP does **not** support a stable TACSTD2–CLDN4 complex in that lysate. PLA proximity ≠ co-internalization with an ADC.

### Szabo et al., 2022 — EPCAM and TROP2 share claudin-7 stabilization

- **PMC** PMC9294608 (retrieved HTML) | *Development*
- **Access:** `full_text`
- Prior binding partners named: claudin-7 primary (Ladwein 2005; Nakatsukasa 2010; Lei 2012).
- In tissues that co-express TROP2 in claudin-7 cells (skin, kidney, bladder, **lungs**), EPCAM loss alone had no or partial effect on claudin-7; combined EPCAM + TROP2 deficiency further suppressed claudin-7.
- Claudin-1 also reduced in some EPCAM-deficient tissues; E-cadherin not affected.
- No ADC internalization data. No CLDN4 binding assay in the retrieved text.

### Wu et al., 2020 — matriptase cleaves EpCAM and TROP2

- **DOI** 10.3390/cells9041027 | *Cells* (retrieved HTML)
- **Access:** `full_text`
- States TROP2, like EpCAM, interacts with claudin-1 and claudin-7 and stabilizes them in corneal epithelium (citing Nakatsukasa).
- Matriptase cleavage destablizes EpCAM/TROP2 and associated claudins. Chloroquine used to test lysosomal degradation after HAI-1/2 loss.
- No CLDN4–TROP2 co-IP and no ADC assay in the retrieved text.

### Zhao / JITC TROP2–claudin immune-exclusion paper (retrieved 2026 HTML)

- **URL** https://jitc.bmj.com/content/14/4/e012265 (retrieved HTML)
- **Access:** `full_text` of the HTML article page
- TROP2 co-IP with claudin 7 in human and murine TNBC cells.
- TROP2-KO tumors: decreased claudin 7 protein and lost membrane localization; decreased claudin 7 and claudin 1 mRNA; occludin membrane staining lost.
- hRS7 (the antibody component of sacituzumab govitecan) is used as a TROP2-targeting agent to enhance anti-PD-1 — **immune-access**, not an internalization-rate study.
- No CLDN4 co-IP number in the retrieved text.

## Proximity without IP

### Fredriksson et al., 2015 — TROP-2 among Cldn4-proximal proteins

- **PMID** 25789658 | *PLoS ONE*
- **Access:** `full_text`
- Quoted: “Some signaling proteins were enriched only around Cldn4. One example is tumor-associated calcium signal transducer 2 (TROP-2; Table 2).”
- Enrichment rule for the table: ≥3-fold vs biotin ligase alone. The TROP-2 row’s numeric abundance was **not** in the retrieved HTML.
- Authors cite Nakatsukasa: TROP2 loss decreases TER and may prevent proper TJ localization of claudins 1 and 7.
- This is neighborhood biotinylation in MDCK II, not antibody-triggered endocytosis.

## What the intersection does **not** show

- No retrieved paper reports TROP2 ADC and CLDN4 in the same endosome or lysosome with a measured overlap.
- Nakatsukasa IP is negative for CLDN4; PLA is positive. Those results should not be collapsed into “TROP2 binds CLDN4.”
- Stabilization of CLDN1/7 by TROP2 is the stronger physical-interaction claim. CLDN4 appears as a localization / proximity partner, not a proven ADC cargo.
