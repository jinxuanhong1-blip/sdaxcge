# TROP2 ADC internalization — catalog notes

Numbers below are copied from retrieved text. Tags: `full_text`, `abstract_only`, `review_cited_unverified_in_primary`.

## Primary antibody internalization (RS7 / EGP-1 / TROP2)

### Stein et al., 1993 — RS7-3G11 in Calu-3

- **PMID** 8253531 | **DOI** 10.1002/ijc.2910550611 | *Int J Cancer* 55:938–946
- **Access:** `abstract_only`
- Murine IgG1 RS7-3G11 raised against human lung squamous-cell carcinoma.
- Internalization into Calu-3 measured with unconjugated MAb plus fluorescence, and with 125I-RS7-3G11 plus acid removal of surface antibody.
- Quoted: “Antibody internalization was noted at 30 min, and by 2 hr virtually all MAb RS7-3G11 was internal.”
- Antigen: glycoprotein Mr 46 kDa; endoglycosidase-F product Mr 35 kDa; purified major band Mr 45 to 48 kDa.
- **Not in this abstract:** a 50% / 1 h half-internalization value.

### Goldenberg and Sharkey, 2018 — review restatement

- **DOI** 10.18632/oncotarget.25615 | Oncotarget review HTML retrieved
- **Access:** `full_text` of the review; cited primaries not re-read
- Quoted: “Particularly interesting was the observation that 50% the RS7 antibody bound to the cell surface internalized within ~1 h [11, 15].”
- Citations [11, 15] are Stein 1993 and Shih 1995 (*Cancer Res* 1995 Suppl 23; 55:5857s–5863s).
- Tag: `review_cited_unverified_in_primary`. Do not treat as a newly measured rate.

## Sacituzumab govitecan (hRS7-CL2A-SN-38, IMMU-132)

### Cardillo et al., 2011 — first hRS7-SN-38 conjugate

- **PMID** 21372224 | **DOI** 10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-10-2939 | *Clin Cancer Res* 17:3157–3169
- **Access:** `abstract_only`
- hRS7 conjugates of CL2-SN-38 and CL2A-SN-38: drug substitution ∼6; cell binding Kd ∼1.2 nmol/L; cytotoxicity IC50 ∼2.2 nmol/L; serum stability in vitro t1/2 ∼20 hours.
- Abstract does not report an internalization percentage or t1/2 for the antigen.

### Goldenberg et al., 2015 — IMMU-132 characterization

- **PMID** 26101915 | **DOI** 10.18632/oncotarget.4318 | *Oncotarget* 6:22496–22512
- **Access:** `full_text` (publisher HTML)
- DAR 7.6:1 (abstract).
- Quoted from HTML: antigen-binding and rapid cell internalization preserved in the ADC; “KD is 0.564 ± 0.055 nM and 0.658 ± 0.140 nM, hRS7 IgG and IMMU-132, respectively.”
- Abstract: “targets up to 136-fold more SN-38 to a human cancer xenograft than irinotecan.”
- HTML also states SN-38 release from the CL2A linker with a half-life of ~1 day (serum). That is linker hydrolysis, not TROP2 endocytosis.

### Cardillo et al., 2015 — broader tumor panel

- **PMID** 25915780 | **DOI** 10.1021/acs.bioconjchem.5b00223 | *Bioconjug Chem* 26:919–931
- **Access:** `abstract_only`
- Binding: “no significant differences between IMMU-132 and parental hRS7 antibody” by flow; SPR showed a binding advantage for IMMU-132.
- Intact ADC mean residence time (MRT) 15.4 h in mice; carrier hRS7 MRT ∼300 h.
- ADCC lost greater than 60% vs hRS7.
- No internalization rate in the abstract.

### Khera et al., 2022 — SG tissue penetration and intracellular release

- **PMID** 36190986 | **PMC** PMC9812893 | *Cancer Res* (retrieved PMC HTML)
- **Access:** `full_text`
- Model: high TROP2 NCI-N87 gastric xenograft; dual-label residualizing dyes.
- Quoted: “Given the relatively fast internalization rate of Trop-2 (t1/2 = 4.16 hours, Supplementary Fig. S2), the extracellular drug release from receptor-bound SG (t1/2 = 21 hours; ref. 14) is likely negligible compared with the amount of SN-38 delivered directly through receptor-mediated internalization.”
- AF680 conjugation to SN-38 “slowed down the rate of linker hydrolysis approximately 3-fold (t1/2 = 58.7 hours, Supplementary Fig. S8).”
- Qualitative: high expression and rapid internalization; 5 mg/kg heterogeneous distribution at 24 h vs 10 mg/kg deeper penetration; flow from n = 3 tumors per dose at 24 h.
- Pulse-chase: 8 h pulse then 24/48/72 h chase; SG γH2AX maximum at 24 h.
- Cites DeVay 2017 that efficient lysosomal trafficking can improve Trop-2 ADC efficacy.

### Goldenberg and Sharkey, 2019 — SG case-study review

- **PMID** 31208270 | **DOI** 10.1080/19420862.2019.1632115 | *mAbs* 11:987–995
- **Access:** `full_text` (publisher HTML)
- Restates rapid internalization of the TROP-2 antibody and SN-38 serum release half-life of ~1 day.
- Mentions RN927C DAR 2.0 and Phase 1 dose range 0.15–4.8 mg/kg (clinical, not internalization kinetics).

## Datopotamab deruxtecan (Dato-DXd, DS-1062a)

### Okajima et al., 2021 — internalization and LAMP-2 trafficking

- **PMID** 34413126 | **PMC** PMC9398094 | **DOI** 10.1158/1535-7163.MCT-21-0206 | *Mol Cancer Ther* 20:2329–2340
- **Access:** `full_text`
- Antibody selected for internalization (Adv-FZ33 / DT3C screens). Target DAR 4. Linker: tetrapeptide, lysosomal protease (cathepsin) cleavage.
- Assay (Austin et al. 2004 method): Alexa Fluor 488–Dato-DXd 10 μg/mL, bind 1 h at 4°C, then 0 / 15 / 30 / 60 / 120 / 180 min at 37°C; surface quench with anti-Alexa488.
- Quoted: “Dato-DXd efficiently internalized with an average rate of 68.5% at 3 hours after binding to the cell-surface TROP2 in all the cell lines tested (Fig. 2A).”
- Quoted: “the internalization rate at 3 hours after binding to the cell-surface TROP2 was more than 50% in all of the cell lines analyzed here.”
- DXd in media at 24 h after 100 nmol/L Dato-DXd vs surface TROP2: Spearman ρ = 0.60.
- Trafficking: BxPC-3 (and NCI-N87 supplement) confocal; time-dependent internalization, perinuclear aggregation, partial colocalization with LAMP-2. Qualitative; no percent colocalization stated in the retrieved text.
- Authors note possible roles for clathrin- or caveolin-mediated endocytosis, lysosomal enzymes, and TROP2 recycling, without measuring those routes.
- PK numbers in the same paper (not internalization): Dato-DXd T1/2 45.12 ± 13.92 h at 6 mg/kg in cynomolgus monkeys; SG T1/2 14.4 ± 3.3 h and 11.7 ± 3.3 h at 8 and 10 mg/kg in humans. Intro also says SG plasma half-life is approximately 11–15 hours.

## Other TROP2 ADCs (internalization language only)

### Strop et al., 2016 — RN927C / PF-06664178

- **PMID** 27582525 | **DOI** 10.1158/1535-7163.MCT-16-0431 | *Mol Cancer Ther* 15:2698–2708
- **Access:** `abstract_only`
- Site-specific Trop-2 ADC, PF-06380101 microtubule inhibitor. IC50 “generally in the subnanomolar range.” In vivo 0.75 to 3 mg/kg.
- Abstract does **not** report internalization % or t1/2.
- King et al. 2018 Phase 1 (PMID from PMC7519583 HTML): “Upon binding to the extra-cellular portion of Trop-2 on the cell surface, PF-06664178 is trafficked to the lysosomes” — qualitative; DAR 2.

### DeVay et al., 2017 — Trop2 vs APLP2 lysosomal delivery

- **PMID** 28151644 | **DOI** 10.1021/acs.bioconjchem.7b00013 | *Bioconjug Chem* 28:1102–1114
- **Access:** `abstract_only`
- Quoted: “a lysosomally targeted ADC against the protein APLP2 mediates cell killing, both in vitro and in vivo, more effectively than an ADC against Trop2, a protein with less efficient lysosomal targeting.”
- No numeric internalization or lysosome-colocalization values in the abstract.
- This is the paper Khera cites for the idea that lysosomal trafficking efficiency can change Trop-2 ADC potency.

## What this arm supports for C1

- TROP2-directed antibodies and ADCs do internalize. Primary time-stamped evidence: Stein 1993 (30 min detectable; nearly complete by 2 h in Calu-3); Okajima 2021 (mean 68.5% of Dato-DXd internal at 3 h); Khera 2022 (authors’ Trop-2 t1/2 4.16 h in their SG assay).
- Lysosomal arrival is shown qualitatively for Dato-DXd (LAMP-2) and is assumed/stated for SG and RN927C. DeVay 2017 argues Trop2 lysosomal targeting is less efficient than APLP2.
- Linker chemistry matters independently of internalization (SG CL2A hydrolysis t1/2 values above).
- No catalogued paper measures whether CLDN4 changes these rates.
