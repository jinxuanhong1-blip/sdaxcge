# C1 gaps (do not invent)

Items below were **not** found as extractable, primary, numeric evidence in the retrieved texts. Do not fill them from memory, blogs, or reviews without retrieving the primary source.

## TROP2 ADC internalization

- No retrieved primary paper reports a single consensus TROP2 internalization half-life that is shared across cell lines and antibodies. Retrieved values differ by assay and reagent (Stein 1993 qualitative time points; Khera 2022 `t1/2 = 4.16 hours` in a supplementary figure of one SG model; Okajima 2021 percent internalized at 3 h for Dato-DXd).
- A secondary source (JACS 2025 LIPTAC paper, retrieved HTML) states TROP2 internalization `t1/2` of approximately 30 min and cites a numbered reference. That primary citation was **not** retrieved here. The 30 min figure is **not** entered in `catalog.tsv`.
- Goldenberg 2018 review states that 50% of surface-bound RS7 internalized within ~1 h and cites Stein 1993 and Shih 1995. Stein 1993 abstract (retrieved) says internalization was noted at 30 min and that by 2 h virtually all antibody was internal; it does **not** state the 50% / ~1 h figure in the abstract. Shih 1995 full text was **not** retrieved. The 50% / ~1 h figure is tagged `review_cited_unverified_in_primary` only.
- DeVay 2017 abstract states Trop2 has “less efficient lysosomal targeting” than APLP2. No percent lysosomal colocalization, recycling fraction, or half-life was present in the retrieved abstract.
- Strop 2016 RN927C abstract does not report an internalization rate. Later reviews state lysosomal trafficking; those reviews are not treated as primary internalization kinetics.
- No retrieved paper reports a numeric comparison of TROP2 internalization in CLDN4-high vs CLDN4-low cells.
- No retrieved paper reports that TROP2 ADC uptake requires CLDN4.
- Per-cell-line internalization percentages behind Okajima’s 68.5% average were not extracted from Fig. 2A (figure values not in the HTML text). Only the stated average and the “more than 50% in all lines at 3 h” sentence are catalogued.
- Khera Supplementary Fig. S2 (the 4.16 h fit) was not independently re-plotted; the number is catalogued as the authors’ stated value.

## CLDN4 trafficking

- No constitutive (unstimulated) CLDN4 internalization half-life was extracted from a primary paper in this catalog.
- Ivanov 2004 shows Ca2+-depletion internalization of CLDN4 into a syntaxin-4 storage compartment that did **not** label as lysosomes. That is not a lysosomal-delivery rate.
- Cong 2015 reports membrane CLDN4 loss from 5 to 60 min after carbachol and a transient cytoplasmic increase at 10 min. The paper does not state a percent internalized or a t1/2 in the retrieved text used here.
- Fredriksson 2015 Table 2 lists TROP-2 as a Cldn4-proximal signaling protein. The HTML dump did not include the numeric average normalized PSM/OPN for TROP-2. No fold-enrichment number for TROP-2 is catalogued.
- No retrieved paper gives a recycling-vs-degradation fraction for CLDN4 after TROP2-antibody binding.

## Intersection (TROP2 × CLDN4)

- Nakatsukasa 2010: TACSTD2 **co-immunoprecipitated CLDN1 and CLDN7, not CLDN4**, in HCE-T-15 lysates. PLA in cornea was positive for TACSTD2–CLDN4. These are not equivalent to ADC co-internalization.
- No retrieved paper measures simultaneous internalization of a TROP2 ADC and CLDN4, or reports a co-trafficking rate.
- No retrieved paper states that CLDN4 is the TROP2 ADC endocytic adaptor.
- TROP2/claudin immune-exclusion papers (JITC 2026 retrieved HTML; Szabo 2022) address barrier/claudin stability, not ADC internalization kinetics.
