DoRothEA human TF–target weights (confidence A, B, C).

Downloaded 2026-08-17 from OmniPath:

  https://omnipathdb.org/interactions?datasets=dorothea&organisms=9606&genesymbols=1&fields=dorothea_level&dorothea_levels=A,B,C

Primary references
- Garcia-Alonso et al., Genome Res. 2019 (DoRothEA)
- Badia-i-Mompel et al., Bioinform. Adv. 2022 (decoupleR)
- Türei / OmniPath (https://omnipathdb.org)

Columns in dorothea_hs_ABC.tsv
- source: TF gene symbol
- target: target gene symbol
- mor: mode of regulation (+1 activation, −1 repression)
- confidence: A (highest) / B / C

mor rule (same convention as decoupleR::get_dorothea when a sign is
missing): consensus_inhibition without consensus_stimulation → −1;
otherwise +1. Unsigned edges are treated as activation, not dropped.

decoupleR / dorothea R packages were not installed in this environment.
The scorer in scripts/analyze.py is the documented weighted-mean
(wmean) estimator from Badia-i-Mompel et al. 2022, not a VIPER NES
and not a permutation NES.
