# Mass wave — CLDN4 knockdown proteomes and claudin-4 interactomes

Public PRIDE and MassIVE inventory for two designs:

1. Protein abundance after CLDN4 / claudin-4 knockdown, knockout, or silencing.
2. Claudin-4 physical or proximity partners (CoIP, BioID, APEX, TurboID).

Open protein tables from the datasets that match were scanned for DNA-PKcs (`PRKDC`), Ku80 (`XRCC5`), Ku70 (`XRCC6`), and STING (`STING1` / `TMEM173`). RAW spectra were not downloaded.

Read `FINDING.md` for the counts. `METHODS.md` records the searches and the files that could not be fetched.
