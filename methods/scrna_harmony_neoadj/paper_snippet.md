# Paper snippet — joint Harmony neoadjuvant scRNA (additive)

*Placement: extra panel after User A3. Do **not** replace the GSE207422 CopyKAT slide.*

Public processed UMI matrices from three neoadjuvant NSCLC series that contain both epithelium and immune cells (GSE207422, GSE241934, GSE291670) were intersected on gene symbols (16,209 shared; 781 used) and integrated with Harmony (batch = dataset). GSE205335 has author malignant epithelium (28,512 cells) but was omitted: it is palliative ICI with RECIST, not neoadjuvant MPR. The joint object contains **429,593 cells** from **66 patients** (Harmony/UMAP used a sample-stratified subset of 77,572 cells). Malignant-like cells were Harmony epithelial clusters after dropping a high normal-lung program; 30 of 63 post-treatment MPR-labeled samples had fewer than 20 such cells and were excluded from tumor-cell tests. Patient-level malignant-like *TACSTD2* was higher in MPR than NMPR but not significant (median 1.90 vs 1.48; n=9 vs 24; p=0.92). *CLDN4* was higher in NMPR but not significant (median 1.34 vs 1.08; p=0.86). Cohort-adjusted OLS for *TACSTD2* (NMPR vs MPR + cohort) gave β=−0.27 (p=0.28). Unadjusted Spearman of malignant-like *TACSTD2* versus joint-embedding T/NK fraction was positive (ρ=0.49, n=32, p=0.0044) and attenuated after residualizing cohort (partial ρ=0.20, p=0.26); *CLDN4* unadjusted ρ=0.59 (p=0.00042), partial ρ=0.39 (p=0.026). The unadjusted positive correlations are driven in part by GSE291670 nuclear RNA, which sits at low *TACSTD2*/*CLDN4* and low T/NK. Where histology was labeled, malignant-like *TACSTD2* LUSC versus LUAD was not significant (n=4 vs 21; p=0.50). These public joint-embedding tests are additive and do not replace User A3.
