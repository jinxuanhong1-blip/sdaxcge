Frozen gene lists in panels.json.

NHEJ_CORE
  Canonical c-NHEJ enzymes from KEGG mmu03450: Xrcc6, Xrcc5, Prkdc,
  Dclre1c, Lig4, Xrcc4, Nhej1, Poll, Polm.
  MRN (Mre11a, Rad50), Fen1 and Dntt stay in the KEGG set only.

STING_CORE
  cGAS–STING machinery on the 2014 mm9 symbols in this table:
  Mb21d1 (Cgas), Tmem173 (Sting), Tbk1, Ikbke, Irf3, Ddx41.
  DNA-PK subunits are left in NHEJ. ISGs are left in IFN_COMPACT.
  Trex1 and Ifnb1 are reported gene-by-gene (both undetected here).

IFN_COMPACT
  Same 26-gene list as methods/gse50927_mouse_cldn4.
  Compact means drop logCPM < 0 (Ifng, Cxcl11 on the naive table).

KEGG_NHEJ_mmu03450, KEGG_CYTOSOLIC_DNA_SENSING_mmu04623
  Enrichr KEGG_2019_Mouse (symbols upper-case; mapped by mouse-case).

REACTOME_NHEJ_R-HSA-5693571, REACTOME_STING_R-HSA-1834941,
REACTOME_CYTOSOLIC_DNA_R-HSA-3134975
  Enrichr Reactome_2022, human symbols mapped to this table.
  REACTOME_STING_NO_NHEJ drops PRKDC, XRCC5, XRCC6 at runtime.

GO_NHEJ_0006303
  Enrichr GO_Biological_Process_2023, GO:0006303.

HALLMARK_INTERFERON_ALPHA_RESPONSE, HALLMARK_INTERFERON_GAMMA_RESPONSE
  MSigDB mh.all.v2023.2.Mm.symbols.gmt (same file as the earlier
  GSE50927 IFN/MHC folder).

  Enrichr KEGG_2019_Mouse. The beyond-cGAS set drops cGAS, STING, TBK1, IKKε,

Slice rule
  NHEJ-core mean logFC (KO minus WT) <= -0.25 and STING-core mean >= +0.25,
  or at least three genes on each side with |logFC| >= 0.5.

Alias map used at runtime (this mm9 table):
  CGAS/MB21D1 -> Mb21d1, STING1/TMEM173 -> Tmem173,
  MRE11 -> Mre11a, TP53BP1 -> Trp53bp1, H2AX -> H2afx.
  IFI16, PAXX, CYREN and the H2BC histone symbols have no row here.
