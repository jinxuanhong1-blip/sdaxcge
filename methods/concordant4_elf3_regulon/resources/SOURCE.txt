DoRothEA human TF–target weights, confidence A, B, and C.

Columns in dorothea_hs_ABC.tsv: source, target, mor, confidence.
mor is +1 (activation) or −1 (repression). Unsigned edges were stored as +1,
the decoupleR convention (Badia-i-Mompel et al., Bioinformatics Advances, 2022).

References: Garcia-Alonso et al., Genome Research 2019; Türei et al., OmniPath.
This is a curated regulon, not a lung ChIP-seq track and not a SCENIC/cisTarget inference.
